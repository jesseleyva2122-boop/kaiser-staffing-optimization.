# Data provenance and complete generation specification

## Origin
This is an AI-assisted Python simulation based on Jesse Leyva's **Final Project Proposal: Nurse Staffing Cost and Coverage at Kaiser Permanente**, provided November 15, 2026. No patient records, actual employee rosters, internal budgets, or Kaiser compensation data are used. Synthetic nurse IDs are integers, not people. The target company is context, not a data supplier or endorsement.

## Design prompt used to produce this generator
“Build a reproducible Phase 1 Python optimization engine for a hypothetical Kaiser Permanente California medical-surgical unit. Use seed 42; 24 beds; 22 licensed, competent RNs; two 12-hour shifts daily for 14 days; 100 candidate staffing plans evaluated against the same 100 census/absence scenarios. Bound census at 12–24 around mean 18; use 5% per-RN per-shift absence and zero/one additional acuity RN. Model an RN-only planning requirement of ceil(census/5), plus acuity and one break-relief RN. Each candidate must cover forecast needs, respect approved availability, at least 12 hours rest and 48 hours weekly; cost hours beyond 36 weekly as overtime. Assume hourly rates $60 regular, $90 overtime, $110 reserved float and at most two qualified floats per shift. Preserve $125,000 as the proposed budget; flag violations rather than claiming they meet it. Use deterministic scenario evaluation and save underlying inputs and assignments. Calculate exact Pareto dominance for lower planned cost and higher coverage reliability; independently validate with a brute-force oracle. Identify balanced, equity, cost-optimal, coverage-optimal and 30/70 weighted frontier strategies using explicitly normalized benefits. Produce three required panels, validation outputs, substantive limitations and an executable notebook. Do not invent observations or claimed clinical outcomes.”

## Exact operational choices
- Master seed 42 generates a fixed 28-shift forecast: rounded Normal(18,1.2), clipped 12–24; forecast extra acuity Bernoulli(0.18).
- 22 nurses are split into 11 Day and 11 Night. All are licensed and unit-competent in this hypothetical roster. Availability is Bernoulli(0.96), restricted to their cohort. Fixed cohorts simplify scheduling and imply at least 12h between shift end and next start.
- Scenario seed 43 creates 100 independent 14-day replications. Census = rounded 18 + Normal(0,2) shared within each day's two shifts + independent Normal(0,1.5), clipped to 12–24. Acuity is Bernoulli(0.35); absence is Bernoulli(0.05) per nurse-shift. Scenarios are independent of candidates. Floats are assumed qualified, guaranteed available, reserved for all 12 hours; correlation in absences or shared float-pool exhaustion is not modeled.
- Candidate seed 44 creates 20 mixed-integer feasible regular rosters. Variant j adds j modulo 7 regular assignments to higher-risk shifts. A 22-by-28 assignment matrix and 44 weekly OT variables implement exact counts, availability and 4-shift weekly caps. Random assignment tie-break costs diversify rosters. Each roster is paired with 0,4,8,14,22 reserved RN shifts, allocated with diminishing marginal risk scores and a per-shift cap of 2: exactly 100 evaluated candidates.
- Candidate rows are the required 50–150 optimization data points. The 2,800 scenario-shift rows and 2,800 candidate-shift assignment rows are supporting simulation inputs, not extra candidate plans.
- Reliability = successful candidate/scenario/shift outcomes / 2,800 ×100. Supply includes present regular RNs and pre-reserved floats. Need includes ratio, acuity and break relief. A shortfall triggers escalation/unplanned replacement, never permission to operate short-staffed. Outcomes are not measured clinical quality or a hospital-wide guarantee.
- Cost includes planned regular, overtime and reserved float hours only. Emergency call-in costs are not included. Weekly OT is a planning assumption, not a complete California wage-law or Kaiser contract model.

## Proposal alignment and deliberate limitations
The proposed RN assignments, float decisions, 14-day horizon, seed, census bounds, scenario count, absence probability, rates, weekly cap and budget are retained. Candidate search is a restricted sampled design, not enumeration of every feasible binary roster; the frontier is exact **within these 100 candidates only**. Phase 2 constraint filtering is not implemented. The budget flag prevents the unconstrained five-strategy table being misread as five feasible recommendations.

The proposal mentioned HCAI 2024 annual utilization for context. The official documentation was reviewed; **no facility-level HCAI numerical extract calibrated this model**. Unit size, mean census, distributions and rates remain declared proposal assumptions. This is an explicit data-source limitation, not a claim that actual Kaiser occupancy is 75%. The HCAI catalog also now lists a 2025 release, but this synthetic exercise does not substitute it silently.

One extra full-shift RN is a coarse break-relief proxy, not an intrashift break schedule or compliance certification. Dedicated cohorts exclude cross-shift flexibility. Absence shocks, acuity and float availability are simplified. An observed 48h weekly cap does not prove compliance with all rolling-window rules. All normal intervals are approximate Monte Carlo uncertainty, not operational guarantees. Both strategies and the frontier can change with candidate sampling, rate assumptions, normalization anchors or a new scenario bank.

## Public sources reviewed November 15, 2026
1. California Department of Public Health, AFL 07-26 (2007): https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CHCQ/LCP/Pages/AFL-07-26.aspx — historical medical/surgical licensed-nurse minimum and acuity context. RN-only coverage is a conservative project choice, not the source's exact formulation.
2. AHRQ PSNet, Nursing and Patient Safety: https://psnet.ahrq.gov/primer/nursing-and-patient-safety — explains why acuity, workload and expertise matter beyond headcount.
3. HCAI, Hospital Utilization: https://hcai.ca.gov/data/healthcare-utilization/hospital-utilization/ — annual facility-level data scope and reporting.
4. HCAI annual data catalog: https://data.ca.gov/@department-of-health-care-access-and-information/hospital-annual-utilization-report-pivot-tables — documents annual utilization releases. No source numbers are represented as synthetic inputs.

## Files and audit trail
`src/engine.py` is the authoritative generator and equations. `data/candidate_plans.csv` contains evaluated outputs, `assignments.csv` identifies every assigned nurse/float, `simulation_inputs.npz` preserves the exact masks and assignments, `shifts.csv`, `nurses.csv`, and `scenarios.csv` provide readable support. `results/validation.json` records actual checks. Solver versions and actual fresh-kernel execution are recorded in `results/reproducibility.json`.
